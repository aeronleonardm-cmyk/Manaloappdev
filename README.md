# My Favorite Series: Breaking Bad ⚗️

**Breaking Bad** is an American crime drama television series created and produced by *Vince Gilligan*. Set and filmed in Albuquerque, New Mexico, it tells the story of Walter White, a struggling, underpaid high school chemistry teacher who is diagnosed with inoperable lung cancer.

> "I am not in danger, Skyler. I am the danger." — *Walter White*

---

## Why It Stands Out
* **Character Development:** Watching Walter White transform into *Heisenberg* is one of the greatest character arcs in television history.
* **Cinematography:** Creative camera angles, color symbolism, and desert landscapes make every episode visually memorable.
* **Tension & Pacing:** Every season builds suspense logically without relying on cheap plot twists.

## Top 3 Favorite Episodes
1. **Ozymandias** (Season 5, Episode 14)
2. **Face Off** (Season 4, Episode 13)
3. **Felina** (Season 5, Episode 16)
